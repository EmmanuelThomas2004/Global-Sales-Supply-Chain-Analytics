# Power BI Dashboards

This folder contains the five Power BI dashboard pages developed for the Global Sales & Supply Chain Analytics project.

The dashboards present insights across executive performance, sales and profitability, customer and market performance, supply chain operations, and strategic recommendations.

## Dashboards

### 1. Global Supply Chain Performance Dashboard
Provides an executive-level overview of sales, profit, orders, customers, products, markets, and delivery performance.

### 2. Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard
Analyzes sales and profitability across product categories, individual products, and global markets.

### 3. Customer & Market Performance Dashboard
Examines customer segments, customer performance, geographic sales distribution, and market contribution.

### 4. Supply Chain & Delivery Analytics Dashboard
Analyzes shipping performance, late deliveries, shipping delays, delivery risk, and shipping modes.

### 5. Key Insights & Strategic Recommendations Dashboard
Summarizes the major findings from the analysis and presents business recommendations based on the identified sales, customer, and supply chain patterns.
